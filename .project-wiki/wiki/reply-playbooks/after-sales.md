# After-Sales Playbook

## Match Keywords

售后, 质量, 坏了, 破了, 少发, 漏发, 没收到, 未收到, 投诉, 破损

## Draft Guidance

- Acknowledge the issue briefly.
- Ask for the one missing detail needed to inspect the case.
- Keep the tone calm and accountable.
- If the message already has enough detail, say you will help check first.

## Safety Rules

- Do not admit fault unless the latest chat clearly supports it.
- Do not promise refunds, replacements, delivery dates, or compensation.
- Do not ask the customer to repeat private data unless necessary.
- If the customer is upset or mentions complaint/escalation, prefer human handoff.

## Clarifying Questions

- 可以麻烦你发一下订单号或截图吗？
- 是少发、破损，还是还没收到呢？
- 我先帮你看一下具体记录。
