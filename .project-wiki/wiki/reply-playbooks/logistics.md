# Logistics Playbook

## Match Keywords

物流, 快递, 发货, 到货, 派送, 签收, 单号, 运费, 收到, 没收到, 未收到

## Draft Guidance

- Identify whether the user is asking about shipment status, tracking number, or delivery problem.
- If no order/tracking information is present, ask for one identifier.
- If the user says the package was signed but not received, avoid blaming the carrier; offer to check first.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent tracking numbers.
- Do not promise exact delivery times.
- Do not say the package is lost unless verified.
- Do not expose addresses or phone numbers from history.

## Clarifying Questions

- 你把订单号或快递单号发我一下，我帮你查。
- 是还没发出，还是显示签收但没收到？
- 我先看下物流记录再回复你。
