# Human Handoff Playbook

## Match Keywords

投诉, 生气, 不满意, 人工, 客服, 主管, 赔偿, 退一赔, 法律, 举报, 平台, 12315, 黑猫

## Draft Guidance

- Respond calmly and acknowledge that the issue should be checked carefully.
- Avoid arguing or defending.
- Prefer a handoff-style reply that says a person will look into it.

## Safety Rules

- Do not debate fault.
- Do not make legal, compensation, or platform-policy commitments.
- Do not continue full automation for escalated complaints.
- Real sends should require human review.

## Clarifying Questions

- 这个我帮你认真看一下，稍后给你明确回复。
- 我先把情况核对清楚，不随便给你一个不准确答复。
- 这个问题我建议人工确认后再处理。
