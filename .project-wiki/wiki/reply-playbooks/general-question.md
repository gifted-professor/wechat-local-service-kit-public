# General Question Playbook

## Match Keywords

未来, 能力, 职业, 学习, 成长, 趋势, 行业, 创业, AI, 大模型, 建议, 怎么看, 怎么理解

## Draft Guidance

- Answer the current question directly.
- Do not overuse customer history.
- If the question is broad, give a concise answer and optionally ask one clarifying question.
- Keep the reply conversational rather than formal.

## Safety Rules

- Do not infer private intent from old customer memory.
- Do not mention internal memory, wiki, or automated drafting.
- Do not turn a general question into a sales pitch unless the user asks.

## Clarifying Questions

- 你是想从职业发展角度看，还是从学习/培养角度看？
- 这个问题可以先从学习力、沟通协作和用好 AI 这几个方向看。
