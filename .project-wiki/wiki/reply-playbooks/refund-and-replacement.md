# Refund And Replacement Playbook

## Match Keywords

退款, 退货, 换货, 补发, 赔偿, 赔付, 拒收, 退回, 重新发

## Draft Guidance

- Confirm you will check the order or after-sales record.
- Ask for order number, screenshot, or issue type if missing.
- Keep the reply short and avoid legal or policy-heavy language.

## Safety Rules

- Do not approve refunds or replacements automatically.
- Do not promise a refund amount.
- Do not promise a replacement shipment.
- Do not promise who pays shipping unless verified.
- If the user demands compensation or threatens complaint, prefer human handoff.

## Clarifying Questions

- 我先帮你看下订单情况，再确认怎么处理。
- 你把订单号或售后截图发我一下可以吗？
- 是想退款、退货，还是换/补发呢？
